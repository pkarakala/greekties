# Greek Ties Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 24, 2026
**Last updated:** July 24, 2026

Greek Ties ("Greek Ties," "we," "us," or "our") provides a private, chapter-scoped
professional networking application for fraternity and sorority members and alumni
(the "Service"), available as a mobile app and companion website. This Privacy
Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and
the choices you have.

If you have questions, contact us at **support@greekties.app**.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** your name and email address, collected when you create
  an account. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase
  Auth) and is never visible to us in plain text.
- **Profile information (optional unless noted):** graduation year, member status
  (active or alumni), industry, company, job title, LinkedIn URL, a short bio,
  whether you are open to mentoring, whether you are hiring, your **city**, and —
  only if you choose to appear on the alumni map — approximate **geographic
  coordinates** for that city.
- **Profile photo (optional):** an avatar image you choose from your photo
  library. We request photo library access only when you initiate this action.
- **User content:** messages you send in chapter channels and mentorship
  conversations, job postings you create, and reports you submit about other
  users or content.
- **Invite and membership data:** the chapter you join, the invite code you used,
  your membership status, and any admin role within your chapter.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Basic technical data** necessary to operate the Service, such as
  authentication session tokens and timestamps on records you create (e.g., when
  a message was sent).
- We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, and we do **not** use analytics,
  advertising, or tracking SDKs in the app.

### 1.3 Location

The map feature uses Mapbox to display chapter and alumni locations. If you grant
location permission, your device location is used **on-device** to center the
map; we do not store your real-time device location. The only location we store
is the optional city/coordinates you enter on your profile.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information solely to provide and protect the Service:

- Creating and maintaining your account and chapter membership.
- Displaying your profile to other members of your chapter (the member
  directory, alumni map, mentorship matching, and job board).
- Delivering messages in channels and mentorship conversations.
- Reviewing reports, enforcing our Terms of Service, and keeping the community
  safe.
- Responding to support requests.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## 3. Who can see your information

Greek Ties is chapter-scoped by design:

- Your profile is visible to **members of your chapter** (and to chapter admins
  reviewing membership requests). It is not public.
- Channel messages are visible to members of that channel. Some channels are
  restricted (for example, executive-board-only or alumni-only channels).
- Mentorship messages are visible only to the two participants.
- Job postings are visible to members of your chapter.
- Chapter admins can see membership information needed to manage the chapter.

## 4. Where your information is stored

Your data is stored with **Supabase** (our hosted backend: database,
authentication, and file storage), on infrastructure operated by Supabase's
cloud providers. Data is protected in transit with TLS and access is restricted
by row-level security policies scoped to your chapter membership.

## 5. Service providers

We share data only with the vendors required to run the Service:

| Provider | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, authentication, file storage | All account, profile, and content data |
| Mapbox | Map rendering | Map tile requests (may include coarse IP-based location, as with any web request) |
| Expo (EAS) | App build and update infrastructure | No personal user data |

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for
their own purposes.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- We retain your data for as long as your account is active.
- **You can delete your account at any time from inside the app** (Me tab →
  Delete Account). Account deletion removes your profile, memberships, messages,
  job postings, and avatar, and deletes your authentication record.
- You may also request deletion by emailing **support@greekties.app**.
- Reports you submitted, or that were submitted about you, may be retained in
  anonymized or minimal form where needed for safety and legal compliance.

## 7. Your rights and choices

- **Access and correction:** you can view and edit your profile in the app.
- **Deletion:** in-app account deletion, or by email (see above).
- **Optional fields:** city, coordinates, photo, LinkedIn, and all professional
  fields are optional — you control what you share.
- Depending on where you live (e.g., the EEA, UK, or California), you may have
  additional statutory rights such as data portability or the right to object to
  processing. Contact **support@greekties.app** to exercise them.

## 8. Children

The Service is intended for college students and alumni and is not directed at
children. You must be at least **17 years old** to use the Service. We do not
knowingly collect information from children under 13; if we learn we have, we
will delete it.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard measures — TLS in transit, database row-level security,
scoped storage access rules, and secure credential storage on device — to
protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure,
so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at
this URL and update the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be announced
in the app.
